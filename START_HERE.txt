PETER (MCGUIRE) — NEXUSDESK ON FEDORA

Double-click Peter.desktop on your Desktop, choose Peter (McGuire) in your
application menu, or run ./start-linux.sh from this directory.

Peter starts the installed VoiceStudio service when needed and connects to your
existing Gemma model in LM Studio. Click the animated bubble or Speak to Peter,
then click again to finish. Ctrl+Space does the same while Peter has focus.
Type into the Home field or open Peter's conversation for source links and undo.

Notes starts empty. Existing calendar/tasks, memories, tools and AI Library use
the original NexusDesk stores. Review actual saved-change summaries and use undo
when a local model misunderstands a request.

For a fresh checkout: ./install-linux.sh, then ./install-voice.sh.
Read README.md, LINUX_PETER.md and PETER_VALIDATION.md for setup, verified checks,
optional AI Library dependencies, and remaining hardware/Wayland limitations.
The old Windows documentation is preserved in docs/LEGACY_WINDOWS.md.

Personal data, credentials, runtimes, weights and backups stay local and are
excluded from source packages and Git. Back up wanted personal data separately.
